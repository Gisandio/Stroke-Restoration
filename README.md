# Visual Cortex Plasticity: Receptive Field Expansion Model

## Overview
Welcome to the Visual Cortex Plasticity repository! This project explores the fascinating world of neural plasticity within the V1 visual cortex. Our focus lies in understanding how receptive fields adapt and expand following a lesion, aiming to restore cortical functionality. By combining computational modeling, neural simulations, and information theory, we delve into the mechanisms underlying post-lesion recovery.

## Key Features
### Receptive Field Models: 
Explore MATLAB implementations of two-dimensional Gaussian receptive fields. Witness their transformation from symmetric to asymmetric as they expand toward the lesion site.
### Plastic Restoration Algorithm: 
Dive into the heart of our research—a novel plastic restoration model. Discover how it orchestrates the recovery process by dynamically adjusting receptive fields.
### Neural Simulations: 
We’ve integrated the Izhikevich neuron model to simulate cortical responses. Investigate how membrane potentials evolve in the perilesional V1 cortex.
### Information Theory Metrics: 
Analyze alterations in entropy, complexity, and information content. Our approach considers the causal relationships between signals using ordinal pattern analysis.

# Folders:
## Information:
The Information folder contains essential functions related to information theory. Specifically, it includes functions for calculating probability distributions using the Bandt and Pompe ordinal pattern method. Additionally, it computes complexity and permutation entropy. These tools are crucial for analyzing the causal relationships and patterns within the data.
Feel free to explore these functions and adapt them to your research needs. If you have any further questions or need assistance with specific aspects, feel free to ask!
